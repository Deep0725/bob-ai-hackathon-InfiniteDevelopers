How PRR is Calculated

The Proportional Reporting Ratio (PRR) measures how frequently a particular adverse event is reported for a specific drug compared to all other drugs.

PRR = [A / (A + B)] / [C / (C + D)]

Where:

  • A = Reports of this drug with this event
  • B = Reports of this drug with other events
  • C = Reports of other drugs with this event
  • D = Reports of other drugs with other events

A signal is flagged when PRR ≥ 2, Chi-square ≥ 4, and Number of reports ≥ 3. These are screening thresholds — flagged signals are statistical associations requiring expert clinical and regulatory review and do not constitute proof of a causal relationship.
